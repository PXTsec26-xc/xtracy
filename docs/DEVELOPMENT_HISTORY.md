## 1. Project Genesis & Vision

XTRACY was conceived in early 2026 by **Elliot** (Sole Founder) under the brand identity **PhantomXTrace (PXT)**. The objective was to address a fundamental defect in consumer and small-organization cybersecurity: the prevalence of "security theater" featuring fake scanning animations, unverified risk scores, and closed-source telemetry.

The platform was built upon four non-negotiable architectural mandates:
1. **Real over Impressive**: Zero fake scans or simulated progress bars.
2. **Deterministic Evidence**: If no data or evidence exists, display `UNKNOWN` or `UNAVAILABLE`.
3. **Local Privacy**: User passphrases and sensitive investigation vaults must be encrypted client-side using WebCrypto without server-side plaintext leakage.
4. **Independent Verifiability**: Any third party must be able to audit and verify digital evidence hashes using standard mathematics (RFC 8785 JCS, SHA-256, ECDSA P-256).
